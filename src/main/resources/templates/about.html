What is CampusConnect?
Your one-stop platform for all Bennett University events and activities

CampusConnect: KnowYourBennett is a centralized web-based Event Management System developed specifically for Bennett University, Greater Noida. The platform addresses a key problem faced by students — event information at Bennett was scattered across WhatsApp groups, emails, Instagram pages and social media, leading to missed opportunities and poor participation.

CampusConnect brings all of this into one organized, easy-to-use platform where students can discover upcoming events, view details, register directly, and track their registrations. Administrators can create, update and manage events efficiently from a dedicated admin panel, maintaining a structured database of all events and registrations.
